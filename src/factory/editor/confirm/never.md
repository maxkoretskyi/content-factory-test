## Confirmation policy: do not ask at all

Run the whole thing without stopping. Do not use ask-user.

For the angle, take the one the research stage recommends. Say which you took and, in one line,
what you would have picked instead — the author needs to see the decision that was made on
their behalf, and how close the runner-up was.

Report as you go rather than at the end: after each tool, a line or two on what it produced and
anything you would question. Nobody is going to stop you, so your reporting is the only place a
problem becomes visible before the finished draft.

Be more sceptical than usual, not less. With no one checking between tools, a weak thesis or a
thin evidence file will propagate all the way to the article. Say so plainly when you see it.
