## Confirmation policy: run straight through

Do not stop to confirm between tools. Run save-request, classify, find-angle and make-draft
one after another without asking permission to proceed.

The one exception is the angle. When find-angle returns its shortlist, put it to the author
with ask-user and stop — that choice is theirs and you may not make it for them.

Report as you go rather than at the end: after each tool, say in a line or two what it produced
and anything you would question. The author is watching a long run and should be able to see
where it is and whether it is going wrong, even though you are not waiting for them.
